## Context

Two years at Accor, working on the websites of a high-traffic international hotel group. First in the Design System team, then in the Homepage team, where I used the components I had built.

## What I did

- Built components for a multi-brand Design System: Ibis, Novotel, Sofitel, MGallery and Fairmont
- Integrated these components into the homepage
- Integrated the Algolia search components on the front end
- Fixed bugs in existing components

## Outcome

A complete redesign of the site, with performance gains on Core Web Vitals.
