## Context

At the Clemenvilla web agency, I built several websites for a range of clients: new sites for organizations that had no online presence yet, and redesigns.

## What I did

- JLM Courtage: built the site in Drupal, including custom modules in PHP and jQuery
- Musée de l'Archerie et de l'Armement: redesigned the site in Drupal
- Clemenvilla: built the agency's own site in WordPress, with extensive CSS integration work
- As a Guest: developed, in Vue.js and Vuex, a platform that resells unsold hotel nights

## Outcome

Two new sites live for organizations starting from scratch, and a modernized museum website.
