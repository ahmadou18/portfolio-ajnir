## Context

Maisons du Monde was redesigning its marketplace. The Nuxt front end communicated with a Magento back end, and part of the content was managed in Prismic.

## What I did

- Developed the front end of the new marketplace in Vue.js and Nuxt
- Consumed data through GraphQL, working closely with the Magento back-end teams
- Integrated editorial content from Prismic
- Built the interface with Sass and Bootstrap

## Outcome

The new Maisons du Monde marketplace went live.
