## Context

At Mazarine, a complete redesign of the Programme and tickets page on the Opéra national de Paris website, the entry point to its ticketing. Alongside this, I also handled maintenance for other agency clients such as Dior, Chanel and Avène, as well as for the Mazarine website.

## What I did

- Fully redesigned the Programme and tickets page
- Developed the templates in Laravel and Blade
- Built the client-side interactions in JavaScript

## Outcome

Once live, the page generated a very high volume of clicks and accompanied a period of record sales.
