## Context

At Mazarine, a redesign of the Nestlé Waters website: a highly interactive experience designed to highlight the brand's commitment to responsible water sourcing in the countries where it operates. The Nuxt front end consumed the API of a headless Drupal. The team had two front-end developers, one of them a creative developer, and two back-end developers.

## What I did

- Developed almost all of the pages and their components, including the homepage
- Built a custom video player with the YouTube IFrame API
- Integrated content from the Drupal API

## Outcome

The site was fully developed, but in the end was not launched, for reasons on the brand's side.
