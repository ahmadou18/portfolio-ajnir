## Context

Ebru Eroksuz supports her clients in cognitive reprogramming, body language and non-verbal communication. She needed a website to present her work to French- and Turkish-speaking audiences.

## What I did

- Built the site with Next.js 16 and TypeScript
- Set up French–Turkish bilingual support
- Integrated Sanity so the client can manage her own content
- Deployed on Vercel

## Outcome

The site is live, with very strong visibility in search engines and in AI answers, and sharply rising traffic.
